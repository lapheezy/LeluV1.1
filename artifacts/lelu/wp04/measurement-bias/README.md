# A measurement bias found and corrected

After the eye appearance and the neutral key light were corrected (dark warm
iris; a smaller, higher key), the face score fell from 24/25 to 18/25, with
EVERY IOD-normalised width up 4-9 % and the inter-canthal distance up 7.5 %
at once while the skull crown stayed at 2.058 IOD: a pupil-distance shift,
not a geometry change.

Detected pupil position along inner -> outer canthus:

| | right | left | IOD px |
|---|---|---|---|
| references (n=6) | 0.526-0.563 | 0.518-0.557 | |
| model, old light/iris (passes 1-4) | 0.528 | **0.613** | 207 |
| model, corrected | 0.517 | 0.529 | 195 |

Under the old large frontal light a catchlight in her left cornea pulled the
detected pupil outward, inflating IOD; fit passes 1-4 were partly calibrated
to that artefact. The corrected measurement matches the references, so the
face is re-fitted under it (pass 5).
