## 7. False barge-in rate

Measured at the decision function (`isEchoUtterance`), over 48 echo returns and
228 genuine-interruption interactions:

| | echo caught | interruptions wrongly discarded |
|---|---|---|
| M22.9 rule (verbatim from `b46b0aa3`) | 48/48 (100%) | **37/228 (16.2%)** — 37.5% of short interruptions |
| M23.0 rule | 48/48 (100%) | **0/228 (0%)** |

Cause: `if (a.length <= 3) return true` treated every fragment of three
characters or fewer as her own trailing audio — including **"no"**. Fixed with
an interrupt lexicon, a question exception, and the original rules unchanged
for actual echo. Regression: `tests/voice-echo-guard.test.ts`.

Acoustic false barge-in remains **BLOCKED**.

## 8. The four silent-turn root causes

Not content-dependent: turns 8/9 asked the same questions as 28/29 and were
answered in the first pass.

1. **Provider refusal spoken as silence.** The M22.9 server log shows every
   provider returning 429 (Groq TPM/TPD exhausted, OpenRouter free daily quota
   exhausted) with retry windows up to 27 minutes. A refusal is a *failure
   notice*, and failure notices were deliberately never spoken — correct
   intent (never read an error as her answer), unexamined consequence: the
   person heard nothing and nothing was recorded.
2. **Superseded turn with no record.** The next question arrived 13.7 s later,
   superseded the stalled turn, and the supersede path recorded nothing.

Classification: categories 3 (stream/completion race), 7 (superseded) and 8
(diagnostic misclassification) apply; 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 do not.

**Fix:** a per-turn outcome ledger with twelve reasons (published at
`window.__leluVoice.turns`), a fixed spoken holding line on refusal
(max once per 15 s, never the provider's error text), and supersession
recorded before the turn is replaced.

## 11. Voice identity findings

The two "failing medium voices" are one defect, and it is not the models:

| | `amy-low` | `amy-medium` | `voice` (alba) |
|---|---|---|---|
| phoneme map | 130 keys, **0 multi-codepoint** | 166 keys, **5** (`aɪ aʊ ɔɪ eɪ oʊ`) | 166, **5** |
| Piper 1.2.0 | works | aborts (`"aɪ" is not a single codepoint`) | aborts |

Piper 1.2.0 looks phonemes up by UTF-8 codepoint; a map keyed by whole phonemes
cannot be addressed that way. `voice.onnx` is a *different speaker* (alba,
en-GB RP), not a duplicate of `amy-medium` (same size, different hash).

Measured on the working voice, same sentence: bright 5.840 s, warm 5.968 s,
calm 6.368 s (calm is 9.0% longer than bright), samples in `audio/`.

**Verdict: LÉLU does not yet have her own voice.** `status: "fallback",
cloned: false`, as the runtime itself reports. No listening evaluation was
possible (no speaker), so no candidate was adopted.

## 12. Personality integration

No parallel personality system. `resolveSpeechDelivery` reads the same
`LeluIdentity` traits, and only traits the identity system considers developed
can move delivery.

30 replies × 10 registers: **30/30 classified as intended, 30/30 resolved to a
named profile, 30/30 kept every meaning-bearing term** when spoken. Register →
profile was identical across the three replies in each register. Developed
playfulness/patience changed delivery on 12/30; a *candidate* trait changed
nothing.

## 13. Resource consumption

30-minute continuous session, all three delivery profiles resident:

* server process steady at **~56–59 MiB**;
* three warm workers at **~112–128 MiB each** (≈346 MiB total);
* per-worker median synthesis 137–146 ms; no queue depth above 0;
* no unbounded growth across the run (see `voice-runtime-resource-report.md`).

All three profiles resident is *necessary*, not wasteful: M22.9 measured that a
warm worker ignores per-request `length_scale`, so a profile change is a
different worker. Idle eviction would trade ~115 MiB for a model reload.

## 14. Recovery results

A worker was killed with `SIGKILL` mid-session. The pool recorded
`worker exited (code null, signal SIGKILL)`, served the next request, and
returned a warm ready worker with a fresh model load within seconds; subsequent
requests were fast again. An earlier run also showed two unplanned worker
replacements under load, both recovered automatically (`modelLoads` 1→3).

## 19. Promotion recommendation

**Do not promote.**

M23.0 is a genuine engineering advance over M22.9 and is safe to keep as a
branch candidate: silence is now explainable and, on refusal, spoken;
interruptions are no longer discarded; regression is clean; resources are
bounded; recovery works.

It is **not** qualified as a demonstrated conversational experience. Four gates
— physical microphone, physical speaker, acoustic echo cancellation, and the
five-minute human conversation — are BLOCKED by an environment with no sound
card, and no simulated evidence was substituted for them. Reducing those gates
to green without a device would be the exact failure this mission was written
to prevent.

Re-score after executing `physical-audio-acceptance.md` and
`five-minute-human-conversation.md` on a machine with a headset.
