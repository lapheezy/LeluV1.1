Approach

Measured, not guessed.

Every surface is checked at four viewports, with keyboard focus and reduced motion respected. A pretty layout that traps a tap on a phone is not finished work.

  1. 01Understand the constraint before drawing.
  2. 02Build the smallest honest version.
  3. 03Measure it on real screens.
  4. 04Ship, then keep it alive.

About
