### 🔎 What’s next?

The remaining issue is that the **panel itself is rendered as a fixed‑position overlay** that sits on top of the app’s header, regardless of the `top` offset. To truly avoid covering the header buttons we need to:

1. **Render the panel as a non‑fixed element** (e.g., `position: absolute` inside the main container) so it respects the header’s layout, or  
2. **Add a top margin / padding to the panel’s content** and lower its `z-index` so the header stays on top, or  
3. **Introduce a separate “mobile‑mode” layout** that places the panel *below* the header rather than over it (e.g., wrap the whole `GenesisWindowFrame` in a container that starts after the header).

These changes would be made in `GenesisWindowFrame.tsx` (or the surrounding layout component) and then re‑validated with the same loop (capture → diff → compare).
