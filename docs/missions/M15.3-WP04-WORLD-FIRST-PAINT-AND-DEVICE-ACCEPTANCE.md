## 1. Root cause of the first-paint freeze

**Trace tooling.** `scripts/avatar/profile-world-first-paint.mjs` records, on one page clock:
- a CPU profile (250 µs), attributed to source and leaf;
- every long task with its start time;
- every WebGL `linkProgram` with the shader name;
- every blocking GL query over 15 ms, per program;
- lifecycle marks: universe, render budget, world data, command issued/resolved, her first world frame;
- a screenshot per stage.

**Before** (`firstpaint/B-before1-desktop.json`, scenario B: world entered behind the Chat Universe, walk typed in chat):

| t after Enter | Event |
|---|---|
| +0.87 s | command issued; Universe closes; render budget `paused → full` |
| +0.90 s | **one 10,152 ms task** begins |
| +11.2 s | the frame loop resumes |
| +13.2 s | arrival confirmed (12.3 s after the command) |
| later | 3.2 s and 2.8 s tasks (`probeGpu`) during her walk window |

Inside the 10.15 s task, by 100 ms bucket:
- shader link waits: `getShaderInfoLog` 4.4 s, `getProgramInfoLog` 1.6 s, `getProgramParameter` 0.9 s;
- texture uploads: `texSubImage2D` 1.7 s, `texSubImage3D` 0.5 s;
- `bufferData` 0.6 s.

**Cause 1: the world's whole one-time preparation lands on its first frame, which is the instant she is asked to act.**
- Under the opaque Universe the world canvas has frameloop `never`, so nothing is compiled or uploaded while it waits.
- Per-program timing (`waits`) shows that ANGLE/SwiftShader resolves a program's link **at its first query, not on submit**. Even after an explicit `flush()`, programs pre-submitted 10 s earlier still blocked 80–700 ms each at first use (`B-after5`).
- So pre-submitting (`renderer.compile`) or `compileAsync` (which needs the parallel extension) cannot remove the wait here.
- Not caused by entering from the chat: scenario A (normal entry) has the same compile cost (4.3 s `getShaderInfoLog`, first frame 4.5 s after the click). Entry from the chat only *moved* all of it onto the command.

**Cause 2: `SystemEnvironment.probeGpu`** (`src/core/cognition/SystemEnvironment.ts`) created a new WebGL context on **every** refresh. The cognitive loop and self-diagnostics both refresh, at 1–3 s each on this renderer: 7.6 s of main thread in 50 s, landing during her walks.

**Programs at first paint** (`links`): 14 world programs and her 5 body programs (`eye_patch_*`, `Material_0*`, `lelu_mouth`: skinned/morph). The morph-target texture (`texSubImage3D`, three.js-internal) is created only at render, so it remains on the first frame.

## 3. Reconciling WP03's 67/68

| Question | Answer (from code and traces) |
|---|---|
| When is the command timestamp set? | `AvatarCommandBus.issue`: `issuedAt = Date.now()`; its 20 s timer is armed there. |
| Where does the deadline start? | At **command issuance**, not at world readiness or her appearance. |
| Is the progress report inside the deadline? | Yes. WP03's presence armed a **fixed 17 s** timer from when it *took* the command, and that timer **resolved** the command ("still on my way"). |
| Response time or arrival time? | The harness step `within-deadline` measures the bus's answer; arrival comes from 1 Hz telemetry. Same page clock (`performance.now`) for both. |
| Why were the 17.1 s and 17.7 s arrivals failures? | She physically arrived inside 20 s, but the command had already been answered at 17.0 s. **Product defect, not a harness defect**: the report pre-empted the deadline it was meant to stand in for. |

**Fix:** F3. The report now fires 400 ms before the bus's own deadline. Expired timers run in due-time order, so it still precedes the bus timeout after a long block.

**Result:** the same step in the three WP04 68-step runs: **arrived and confirmed at 13.7 s, 13.1 s and 11.1 s** (WP03: 15.3–17.0 s, 2 of 5 answered by a report).

3. The morph-target texture of her face is still uploaded on the first frame (three.js-internal).
4. The total WebGL context count rises by 4 per world entry (live canvases stay at 2): Cosmos/world lifecycle, recorded, not changed.
5. On a phone the Home navigation is not shown inside her world; the exit is the pill → "◂ Genesis v1" (now reachable).
