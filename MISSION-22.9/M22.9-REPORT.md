## 4. Persistent-worker architecture

One long-lived `piper --model … --json-input` process **per delivery profile**,
fed one JSON line per sentence, answering with a WAV file whose header is
patched last (the completion signal). Requests serialise per worker and run in
parallel across profiles; queue bounded at 8; 20 s per-request ceiling; a
barged sentence is abandoned without losing the model; a crashed worker
rejects its request and is replaced on the next one; a worker that dies before
ever answering is reported as a model-load failure.

## 5. Model initialisation behaviour

**20 sentences → 1 model load, 1 PID, 21 requests** (test), and in the live
server 30 browser turns → 3 loads total because three delivery profiles were
used. Measured `modelLoads` never grows for repeated sentences within a
profile.

profiles → the warm worker's own flags. Four dimensions stay separate: identity
(`voiceIdentity.ts`), style (her cognition), emotion (her developed traits +
the reply's register), delivery (`speechProfile.ts`). 30 representative
replies tested across 10 registers; a candidate trait changes nothing until it
is promoted; measured 5.10 / 5.24 / 5.60 s on one sentence across profiles.
